The KOB (Kitchen Owner Business) campaign targets **home cooks** across South India, inviting them to join Shero's platform and launch their own food brand on Swiggy & Zomato.

**Offer:** ₹7,000 Kitchen Partner Activation Package
- Shero Registration

- Swiggy & Zomato Listing + FSSAI
- Recipe Chart & Certificate
- ₹10,000 Deposit — Waived

**Target Audience:** Home cooks aged 18+, Tamil / Telugu / Malayalam speaking

## Key Observations

### What's Working
- **High CTR of 2.57%** — Industry benchmark for lead gen is 1–2%. We are above average.
- **Low CPC of ₹3.36** — Very cost-efficient reach for vernacular audiences.
- **3-language targeting** — Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam covers our highest-density KOB markets.
- **All 3 ads are approved** — No policy issues or creative rejections.

### Areas to Watch
- **Malayalam ad set is paused** — Reactivating it could increase lead volume by ~30%.
- **Budget utilization is low** — Only ₹825/day spent vs ₹2,000 cap. Algorithm has room to scale.
- **Conversion quality** — 1,38,504 conversions at ₹0.15 CPA suggests video views are being tracked as conversions. Recommend verifying actual lead form submissions separately.
