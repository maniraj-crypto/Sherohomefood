**Steps to set in Google Ads UI:**
1. Open campaign "DST - Non Veg Party - Chennai" (ID: 23908833746)
2. Go to Settings → Locations
3. Click + Add location → search "Chennai" → Add
4. Click on "Location options" → change to **"Presence: People in your targeted locations"** (Presence only mode)
